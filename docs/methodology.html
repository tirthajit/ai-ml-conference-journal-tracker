Source policy

Methodology

How venues, ranks, deadlines, and journals are selected and labelled.

Scope

This tracker focuses on reputable archival conferences and journals in artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, NLP, data mining, information retrieval, robotics, medical imaging, HCI/AI, responsible AI, and adjacent computational research areas.

Inclusion criteria

  • Recognized academic venue or journal with archival publications.
  • Clear relevance to AI/ML or adjacent computational research.
  • Verifiable homepage, CFP, publisher page, ranking database, or deadline tracker.
  • Useful to researchers planning submissions.

Exclusion criteria

  • Predatory or unverifiable conferences.
  • Generic commercial events without archival proceedings.
  • Workshops unless they are unusually central to the field.
  • Rows without enough provenance to support public use.

Deadline confidence

Official announced means the date is verified from an official venue page or CFP. Tracker labels indicate reputable secondary tracking. Tentative labels indicate historical inference or annual-window estimates. Rolling labels require manual checking because there may be multiple deadlines or cycles.

Source hierarchy

  1. Official conference, journal, publisher, or society page.
  2. Recognized ranking or indexing source.
  3. Reputable deadline tracker.
  4. Historical inference, always marked tentative.
